# The World Today — 20 November 2026

Entry for make.fun's **"Sum up 20 November's world news in one image"**
bounty ($250 in USDC).

A pure-stdlib Python program (`main.py`, Python 3.13, no third-party
packages, no network) that draws a newspaper front page summing up the
world news of Friday, 20 November 2026 as a single 1024x1024 PNG.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png (default /output/entry.png)
```

Deterministic: two runs write byte-identical PNGs (23,127 bytes).

## The front page

- **Lead:** Trump, Xi and Putin hold the first-ever trilateral summit in
  Shenzhen, China, and sign a joint peace declaration on nuclear arms,
  energy security and the world's conflicts.
- **The Hague:** the ICJ rules Myanmar's military likely intended to
  destroy a substantial part of the Rohingya in the 2017 crackdown.
- **Seoul:** South Korea's Supreme Court overturns ex-PM Han Duck-soo's
  conviction in the case tied to the 2024 martial law declaration.
- **Suva:** Fiji declares a national state of disaster as El Nino drought
  brings severe water shortages.
- **Kyiv:** a Ukrainian attack on the Crimean Bridge halts rail traffic;
  Russian strikes damage the Austrian and Dutch embassies.
- **Vienna:** the IAEA reports North Korea is expanding its uranium
  enrichment facility at Yongbyon.
- **Caracas:** the US and Venezuela sign a deal to restore military
  cooperation, under a year after Maduro's ouster.
- **Also:** Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4m; Cyclone Akvan bears down
  on Odisha; Nicholls' century lifts New Zealand in Wellington; Anthropic
  valued at $1.5 trillion.
